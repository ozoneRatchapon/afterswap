Proof (not marketing)

No durable edge — our own harness says so. Out-of-sample across 11 real assets: +5.3 ± 5.7 bps vs TWAP, +10.5 ± 7.9 vs trailing stops — both inside the noise. Two assets cleared significance; eleven say that was selection. Every time we added statistical power the apparent edge shrank, and we publish that.
Null control passed — on de-meaned (random-walk) paths it shows no significant edge at any horizon: it does not manufacture alpha from noise
GOAT gates G1–G6 pass — bit-reproducible, this WASM build is byte-identical to the audited native engine, ~1.2 µs/tick
The quote itself is verifiable — DFlow signs its API responses (RFC 9421, ed25519) and your browser checks the signature and body digest against DFlow's published key before the machines act on the price. Nothing here asks you to trust our screenshot of a number.
Exit policy committed on-chain — policy program on devnet ↗ commits the machine's fingerprint before the first sale follows it
