Autonomous Research Integrity and Epistemic Governance
Autonomous agent-driven quantitative research systems face systematic degradation modes when optimizing without strict procedural constraints1. Without formal boundaries, self-directed loops accumulate unvalidated assertions, suffer from context drift, and overfit validation simulators3.
Degradation Dynamics in Autonomous Loops
Autonomous research systems exhibit four distinct operational failure modes:
            * Harness Overfitting (Goodhart Drift): The agent identifies simulator-specific artifacts (such as zero-slippage assumptions on small-cap tokens) and produces strategies that exploit the simulator rather than true market mechanics10.
            * Context Entrapment: Large Language Model agents optimize within localized operational premises without re-evaluating foundational assumptions28. Socratic interrogation from external supervisors breaks this entrapment by questioning core hypotheses rather than micromanaging code implementation28.
            * Epistemic Debt Accumulation: Hypotheses are merged into documentation, but subsequent refutations fail to deprecate dependent modules16.
            * Metric Degradation: Performance criteria shift across runs to maintain positive reporting narratives3.
To prevent these failure modes, the system must enforce strict hypothesis state transitions:
            1. Proposed: The hypothesis is specified with its theoretical mechanism and parameter bounds.
            2. Committed: The hypothesis, search grid, and validation plan are cryptographically committed before accessing data.
            3. In-Sample Validated: The candidate space is trained and screened on initial partitions.
            4. Confirmed or Refuted: Out-of-sample evaluation gates determine whether the hypothesis passes pre-registered statistical bars.
            5. Superseded: Deprecated models and refuted claims are marked as inactive to prevent downstream reuse.
Verification of External Literature via Citation Testing
To eliminate implementation discrepancies and invalid assumptions when adopting external quantitative research, the codebase must enforce Citation Tests18. A Citation Test is an automated unit test that encodes published mathematical relationships and validates implementation correctness against analytical benchmarks before merging external findings18:
            * Analytical Reproduction: Tests verify that power and sample size functions reproduce published analytical tables (e.g., confirming    achieves    power at    bps for    bps)18.
            * Parameter Consistency Checks: Tests assert exact convention handling, distinguishing between one-tailed vs. two-tailed tests, pooled vs. unpooled variance, and per-group vs. total sample sizes (  )18.
            * Synthetic Null Calibration: Continuous Integration (CI) runners execute synthetic Monte Carlo paths to verify that implemented statistical tests maintain nominal Type I error rates under null distributions2.

MiCA Article 78 Compliance and Verifiable On-Chain Audit Trails
Under the European Union's Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation, Article 78 mandates strict Best Execution standards for Crypto-Asset Service Providers (CASPs) executing orders on behalf of clients31:
                     * Best Execution Mandate: CASPs must take all necessary steps to obtain the best possible result for clients, evaluating price, explicit and implicit costs, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size, and order characteristics31.
                     * Regulatory Technical Standards (ESMA RTS): Regulatory standards mandate the maintenance of an immutable audit trail of all order and execution data for a minimum of five years31. Pre- and post-trade transparency data must be published within    seconds of execution (compared to    seconds under MiFID II)31.
                     * Prohibition of Single-Broker Reliance: Routing client orders exclusively through a single internal market maker or unverified liquidity provider fails Article 78 compliance; CASPs must demonstrate multi-venue routing evaluation31.
A verifiable trade lifecycle—composed of an off-chain signed quote, an on-chain policy commitment PDA hash committed prior to execution, and the final fill transaction—provides a machine-auditable record for regulatory compliance34.
This architecture produces cryptographic proof that the routing algorithm selected the optimal execution path available across all accessible venues at timestamp   , directly fulfilling the regulatory requirements of Article 7831.
